About Syed Faiz Afroz

Syed Faiz Afroz is an independent journalist, political commentator, and author whose work focuses on geopolitics, international affairs, and the stories shaping today's world.

He believes journalism is at its best when it asks difficult questions, challenges those in power, and puts facts before narratives. His reporting and analysis are driven by a commitment to helping readers understand complex global issues with clarity and context.

As an author, Syed Faiz Afroz explores history, politics, and international relations beyond the transient news cycle, sparking meaningful conversations and critical thinking.
