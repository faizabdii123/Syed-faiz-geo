ABOUT THE JOURNALIST

Independent voices matter.

I'm Syed Faiz Geo, an independent journalist, political commentator and author whose work focuses on geopolitics, international affairs and stories shaping today's world.

I believe journalism is at its best when it asks difficult questions, challenges those in power and puts facts before narratives.

My work is driven by a simple goal: to make complicated global events easier to understand while questioning the assumptions behind the headlines.

“Journalism should not simply repeat the headline. It should ask what came before it, who benefits from it, and what comes next.”
