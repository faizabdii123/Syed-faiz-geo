Turning Subway Heat Into Energy

NYC is literally turning subway heat into a heating system. 🔥🚇

Mayor Mamdani’s plan would capture excess heat from subway stations, use it to cool the stations, then repurpose that heat to warm municipal buildings in winter.

And yes, officials say it would be the first thermal energy system of its kind in any American transit system.
